Find your next corporation, or your next recruits

Eve-Corps.online is an out-of-game directory of EVE Online corporations that are recruiting. Browse, filter and get in touch, without having to log in first.

Looking for a corp?

  • Filter by timezone, language, activities and where they live, from high-sec to wormhole space.
  • Narrow it down to new-player friendly corps, ship replacement (SRP), alliance or independent, and skill point requirements that fit you.
  • Search corp and alliance names, tickers and advert text.
  • Check a corp out before you apply, with links to zKillboard, EveWho and DOTLAN, then join their Discord or in-game channel, or contact a recruiter directly.
Find a Corp

Recruiting?

  • Log in with EVE Online. CEOs, directors and members with the Personnel Manager role can manage their corporation's advert.
  • Write it in Markdown, or start from your in-game corporation description or a pasted in-game advert.
  • Add your Discord, website, recruitment channel and recruiters, so pilots know exactly where to go.
  • Whenever one of your recruiters logs in or edits the advert, it shows as recently updated, so pilots can see you're still active.
Put up a Corp Advert

It's free to use. We only ask EVE for your corporation roles, never your wallet, assets or mail, and we don't keep your login token.
